The Bridge That Actually Scores: Office Kit

Why custom WebSockets lose and how Nexus captures the 10% HackTracker score.

The Nexus Innovation

Nexus serializes tasks as TaskDescriptor JSON and writes to clipboard. Office Kit syncs it natively to the laptop.
